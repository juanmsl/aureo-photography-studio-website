## Platform

web

## Stack

React 19 + TypeScript + Vite, Polpo UI library, Tailwind CSS v4, pnpm 11 and Node 24. Design in Figma (Áureo's own file).

## Users

People, brands, publishers and projects looking for photography with a point of view, in any of the studio's disciplines. They come to see work, understand how the studio looks at things and decide whether to start a conversation. Also people who just want to contemplate and discover photographs, and perhaps buy them.

## Product Purpose

Áureo Estudio Fotográfico is the brand of the creative and photography studio. The site presents its way of observing and revealing the world, shows work across several disciplines and opens the conversation for commissions. Success: the visitor feels inspired and curious, wants to keep looking and ends up writing or asking about a photograph.

## Positioning

The signature is not a photographic genre but a way of looking: revealing the value that exists beyond the obvious (the "áureo": value, harmony, permanence) by crossing the threshold between the visible and the unnoticed. Approved slogan: «Lo extraordinario, más allá de lo evidente.»

## Operating Context

It lives alongside Lina Nieto's personal portfolio, which is a separate project and is not modified. Áureo is the studio brand; Lina Nieto is the author's signature. How they link to each other (navigation, footer, "about the studio") has not been decided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Disciplines: nature, landscape, macro photography, portrait, fashion, architecture, interiors, events and product. None of them should dominate the identity.
- Logo not designed yet: it will come later, in several versions (logotype, symbol, monogram, horizontal/vertical, mono, light/dark). No cameras, lenses or shutters.
- The golden ratio may inspire the construction, but it is not mentioned in the brand and the spiral is not used as an obvious ornament.
- Preliminary palette, not approved: ink black #171C20, petrol blue #234B55, emerald green #28564A, wine #642F43, ivory #F0E9DF.
- Final typefaces and palette: pending.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Áureo** (with the accent). Descriptor: **Estudio Fotográfico**.
- Slogan: «Lo extraordinario, más allá de lo evidente.» (do not change without asking). Allowed secondary phrase: «Donde cada detalle revela un mundo.»
- Approved brand statement: see `docs/CONTEXT.md`, section 6, approved version.
- Personality: artistic, sensitive, curious, sophisticated, timeless, versatile, contemplative; slightly ethereal without losing clarity.
- Avoid: grandiloquence, empty poetry, repeating light/details/worlds, being boxed into one genre, ostentatious luxury.
- User references (moodboard, November 2026): warm chiaroscuro, golden light crossing a door or an arch, backlit leaf veins, eyes crossed by a band of light, a curled fern.

## Evidence on Hand

- Moodboard reference images in `public/photos/` (they are not the studio's own photographs; they are used as provisional and must be labeled as such).
- There are no clients, testimonials, prices or awards: do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The way of looking is the product; the disciplines are examples of it.
2. Restrained content: little text, every word earns its place; nothing redundant.
3. Áureo and Lina Nieto complement each other and never replace one another.
4. Elegance without distance: sophisticated yet approachable and clear.
5. Concept is settled before visuals: do not treat as approved what was only explored.
